RackStat Enclosures

Modular PVC vivariums and terrariums with interchangeable roofs, so you can adapt the enclosure as your setup changes.

Concept in development

One enclosure. A choice of roofs.

The walls, base and front would bolt together. A common roof fitting would let you swap the top panel, with locating pegs to line it up. We’re working through how each roof would secure to the body.

Body
Flat-pack, bolt-together PVC panels
Doors
Sliding or hinged glass, with lightweight acrylic in consideration for smaller builds

Roof options in development

Solid PVC

A solid top, with integrated lighting among the options we’re exploring.

Mesh

Full, half and quarter mesh concepts for setups with different ventilation and lighting needs.

Dome cut-outs

A top panel with a dedicated opening for a lamp dome.
