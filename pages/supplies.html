Reptile nutrition · In research and development

Getting gecko food right

When a food my geckos eat eagerly goes out of stock in the UK, finding another I’m happy to feed them can be hard. I’ve tried alternatives that my geckos showed little interest in, and I wanted more from a food I’d use regularly.

We’re developing our own recipes at RackStat Supplies, starting with a fig-and-insect gecko diet. The aim is food our geckos are keen to eat and UK keepers can get hold of reliably. We’re working on recipes around the animals’ nutritional needs, exploring organic ingredients where they suit the recipe, and looking at how consistently the food mixes and holds up once prepared.

We’re also exploring individual ingredients and feeding blends for other reptiles and exotic pets.
