RackStat Racks

Flat-pack PVC racks designed for keepers who work with their tubs every day.

In design development

The V70 rack concept

The rack splits into a six-level base and a three-level extension, with a slide-out worktop above level six and a castor base to help move the rack when needed.

Layout
18 V70 tubs, two across and nine levels high
Heating
Recessed heat mats in three zones, with dedicated probe positions
Ventilation
Front ventilation channel with restrictor plates under consideration
Assembly
Bolt-together PVC panels

A prototype build will be used to check fit, heating and how the rack works in daily use.

Planned tub formats

We’re exploring a mixed V18 and V35S rack, a dedicated V35 rack and the V70 design for larger tubs.
